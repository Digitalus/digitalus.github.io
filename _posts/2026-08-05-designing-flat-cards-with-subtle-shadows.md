Heavy gradients and thick borders can make a page feel busy. For this site, I switched to flat cards with a thin border and a soft shadow.

The pattern is simple:

- A light border in a neutral gray.
- A small shadow that lifts on hover.
- Generous padding, usually around 24px.
- Consistent corner radius across the page.

Whitespace does most of the work. When content has room to breathe, the design looks clean without extra decoration.
