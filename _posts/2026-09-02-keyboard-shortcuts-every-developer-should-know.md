Learning a few shortcuts pays off quickly. These are the ones I use constantly:

- **Ctrl+P** (or Cmd+P): jump to any file by name.
- **Ctrl+Shift+F**: search across the whole project.
- **Alt+Up/Down**: move the current line.
- **Ctrl+`**: toggle the integrated terminal.
- **Ctrl+R** in a shell: search your command history.

Pick two or three per week and practice them until they feel automatic.
