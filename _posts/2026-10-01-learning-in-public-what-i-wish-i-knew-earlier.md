For years I kept my learning private. I thought I had to be an expert before sharing anything. That was a mistake.

Writing short notes about what I'm learning has taught me more than any course. It forces me to explain ideas clearly, and it has connected me with people who correct my mistakes.

You don't need a big audience. Start with one post a month, and focus on what you found confusing. Someone else probably finds it confusing too.
